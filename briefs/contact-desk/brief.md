# Brief: the desk in front

**Where it lives:** the same corner as the greeting, drawn over him.
**Type:** static illustration, one layer, always in front.
**The idea in one sentence:** the desk that hides his legs and makes standing up
free.

**Why it is its own asset.** It is an occluder. Everything the animation does not
want to draw goes behind it, so it has to be opaque, wide enough to cover him at
his lowest point, and drawn from the front with nothing of its top surface
showing.

**Framing:** landscape, 1024x576. The desk fills the width and sits on the
bottom edge.

**Required:** a monitor seen from behind, in near black, standing on a desk. No
screen is visible, because the screen faces away.

**Forbidden:** a visible screen, a keyboard seen from above, a person, a chair,
a ground shadow, text.

## Prompt

A wide desk seen straight from the front, filling the width of the picture, with
a matte near-black computer monitor standing on it. The monitor is seen from
behind: a plain dark rectangle on a narrow stand, with a slight bulge in the
middle of its back panel and a cable leaving its lower edge and curving down
behind the desk.

The desk top is a straight horizontal band of brown wood, and below it the front
panel is one flat area of the same wood, unbroken from side to side.

Everything else is plain empty off-white paper.

## Note on the control section

Used when a control image supplies the composition, which is not the normal case
for this asset.

## Prompt with structure

A brown desk seen from the front with a matte near-black monitor on it, the
monitor seen from behind with no screen showing, one cable curving down. Flat
even colour, plain empty paper around it.

## Acceptance

- The monitor's screen is not visible from this angle.
- The desk's front panel is opaque and unbroken across the whole width, so
  anything behind it is hidden at any height.
- Every filled area is flat, with no gradient and no cast shadow.
- The colours are the ones in the spec's Tokens table and no others.
- No readable words appear anywhere.

**Budget:** 1024x576, drafts at 12 steps.
**Estimated cost:** zero. Local, `IMAGE_BACKEND=local`.
